What is DMIT?

  1. What is DMIT?

    • DMIT stands for Dermatoglyphics Multiple Intelligence Test.

    • A fingerprint-based assessment that helps you understand your natural strengths, learning style, personality traits, and career potential.

  2. How will it help me or my child?

    • Know your natural talents

    • Discover your dominant intelligence

    • Improve learning or work style

    • Make smart career decisions

    • Reduce confusion and boost clarity

  3. Is this like astrology or palm reading?

    • Nope! DMIT is completely scientific, based on brain research and fingerprint patterns.

    • It's not predictions or beliefs.

  4. Who can take this test?

    • Anyone above the age of 3! Whether you're a child, student, working professional, or parent — DMIT is for anyone seeking self-understanding.

  5. Is it a written exam?

    • No writing, no studying! It's just a simple fingerprint scan that takes 10-15 minutes. That's it!

  6. How much time does it take?

    • Fingerprint scan: 10-15 minutes

    • Report & counselling: Scheduled as per your convenience.

  7. Is my data safe?

    • 100% confidential and secure. Your fingerprint data is used only for your analysis — nothing else.

  8. How do I get started?

    • Just tap the Book a Session button or message us. we'll guide you step-by-step!
